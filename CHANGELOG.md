## 1.1.0 — 2026-09-08

Section 11, the construction-phase workforce plan, from the first community
contribution.

- **added** `s11` — Plan the construction phase before the first truck arrives.
  Seven requirements: workforce curve disclosure, staging and laydown siting,
  delivery and shift timing, funded transit, road baseline plus remediation bond
  before mobilization, a named accountable workforce manager under a code of
  conduct, and EMS funding tied to measured call load. Proposed in the public
  discussion of the source post.
- **added** `ev-construction-phase-impacts` — the section's central claim,
  uncited until the contributor's public-case reference is attached.
- **changed** questions — "What will be built?" and "What will it consume?" now
  count Section 11 in their readiness.
- **added** `api/v1/changelog.json` — this, as an endpoint.
